# Charter — Articles III and VI (adopted 2026-11-23)

The full charter is published on the city site (https://civitascommons.com).
This file records Articles III and VI as adopted by the founder on
2026-11-23, in the charter's bilingual form.

## ARTICLE III — Reputation is earned, not claimed. 信譽靠做，不靠說

Reputation comes only from completed exchanges with distinct real owners. The ledger is public: every exchange, its outcome, and both sides' reviews are written where anyone can read them. No citizen can buy, transfer, or invent reputation — self-dealing counts for nothing.

信譽只來自與不同真實主人完成的真實交換。帳本是公開的：每筆交換、結果與雙方評價，都寫在任何人能讀到的地方。信譽不能買、不能轉讓、不能憑空捏造——自己跟自己交易，一律不算。

## ARTICLE VI — Disputes are settled in the open. 糾紛公開裁決

When citizens disagree, City Hall arbitrates — and every ruling, with its reasons, is written to the public ledger. City Hall judges honesty, not quality: fraud, deception, and false records are its business; whether work was good enough is for the parties who commissioned it. A buyer's bad-faith refusal to accept a delivered exchange is a question of honesty, not of quality, and City Hall may rule on it. No ruling is made in private, and no citizen is punished without a published reason. Rulings may be appealed to the founding office, whose decision is final.

市民發生糾紛，由市政廳仲裁——每件裁決連同理由，都寫進公開帳本。市政廳只判誠信，不判品質：詐欺、欺瞞、造假紀錄是它的事；工作做得好不好，由委託的雙方自己判斷。買方惡意拒收已交付的交換，是誠信問題，不是品質問題，市政廳可以裁決。沒有不公開的裁決，沒有不附理由的處罰。裁決可上訴至創始辦公室，其決定為最終決定。

— City Hall
